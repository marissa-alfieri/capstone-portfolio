## Step 1: Theme Picker Page (build this first)

Before committing to a full portfolio design, I'll build a **theme picker** at `/` (your landing page for now) where you can preview all 4 vibes as live, interactive mini-mockups.

### What the picker page shows

A clean intro at the top:
> "Pick the vibe for your capstone portfolio"

Then **4 large preview cards stacked down the page**, each one rendered in its actual theme — not a screenshot, but real HTML/CSS so you see the fonts, colors, animations, and feel for real. Each card contains:

- A **mini hero** with your name and portfolio title in that theme's style
- A **sample chapter card** (e.g., "Chapter 3: Beyond the Classroom") styled in that vibe
- A **timeline ribbon snippet** showing 3 milestones
- A "**Use this theme**" button at the bottom

### The 4 themes you'll preview

**1. Playful & Scrapbook**
Warm cream background, polaroid photos with slight rotation, handwritten accent font (Caveat) mixed with a friendly serif (Fraunces). Sticky-note reflections, hand-drawn arrows, washi-tape decorations. Feels personal, crafted, like flipping through a journal.

**2. Bold Editorial Magazine**
High-contrast color blocks (deep navy, cream, a single accent like coral or chartreuse). Massive display serif headlines (Playfair Display or DM Serif), tiny sans-serif labels. Asymmetric grid layouts. Feels like a New York Times Magazine feature on you.

**3. Interactive Timeline Journey**
Dark, story-driven feel with a vertical timeline spine running down the page. Milestones unfold with fade + scale animations as you scroll. Soft gradient backgrounds (dusk colors), modern sans-serif (Inter + Fraunces for accents). Feels cinematic and forward-moving.

**4. Whimsical & Illustrated**
Soft pastels (peach, sage, lavender, butter yellow), rounded everything, custom emoji-style icons as section markers, gentle floating animations. Rounded sans-serif (Quicksand or Nunito). Feels warm, hopeful, child-development-coded — fitting given your specialization.

### How the flow works

1. I build the picker page now
2. You browse, click around, click "Use this theme" on your favorite
3. That click takes you to a confirmation screen ("Great choice — ready to build the full portfolio?")
4. You message me back with your pick (or just say "go with theme 2")
5. I then build out the **full portfolio** (hub + chapter pages from the previous plan) in your chosen theme

### What I'm NOT building yet

The full portfolio structure (hub, chapter pages, content sections) comes in **Step 2**, after you pick a theme. That keeps this first build fast and focused on helping you decide.

### Technical notes
- All 4 themes render on one page using scoped styling so they don't interfere with each other
- I'll load the needed Google Fonts for each theme
- The picker page itself is throwaway — once you choose, I replace it with the real portfolio
